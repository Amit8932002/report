\SpecialTitle{Abstract}

\noindent
This report documents the internship project titled \textit{Project ION Migration}, undertaken at Juspay Technologies Pvt.\ Ltd.\ as part of the Breeze team. The project addresses the systemic migration of Juspay's checkout frontend from a legacy Hyper-widget architecture (built in PureScript) to a modern, Svelte-based ION framework. The migration spans three principal dimensions: performance improvement, Payment Card Industry (PCI) compliance, and user experience modernisation.

\vspace{4mm}
\noindent
Three concrete technical contributions are detailed. The first is the design and implementation of a \textbf{PCI-compliant automated CI/CD pipeline} that replaces manual deployment workflows with a fully automated system for publishing TypeScript-based modules to AWS S3. The second contribution is the \textbf{DOTP (Device OTP) module}, which eliminates external bank-hosted OTP redirects by rendering a native, on-page OTP capture interface within the merchant's checkout session, thereby improving conversion rates and security posture through 3DS 2.0 compliance. The third contribution is the ongoing \textbf{EMI Flow Integration}, which remaps the EMI processing engine to post-migration card identifiers, ensuring schema parity and continuity of transaction success rates.

\vspace{4mm}
\noindent
The report further describes Juspay's broader product ecosystem, the architectural rationale for the ION migration, and the performance gains achieved through reduced I/O, smaller asset bundles, and improved browser compatibility.
